Mine Monero and Cryptoescudo
from one app.

minerfan runs its own P2Pool node for Monero and mines Cryptoescudo on your graphics card, both at once. Your coins go straight to your own wallets, which the app creates for you. No pool operator, no account, no servers.

What it does

Monero on your own P2Pool node

The app runs a P2Pool node (the mini sidechain by default) and its own light peer on the Monero network. When any P2Pool miner finds a block, the block itself pays every wallet with a share in the window, yours included. Nothing to collect, no pool fee.

RandomX, written in Dart

On x86-64 and ARM64 a port of xmrig's RandomX compiler turns each program into machine code at run time; other devices use a plain interpreter. No monerod, no p2pool, no xmrig binaries are bundled or downloaded.

Cryptoescudo on the GPU

Solo mining of Cryptoescudo (CESC) with an OpenCL scrypt kernel, on its own light node. The effort slider keeps you to a fair share of this small network (25% by default). Each block you find pays its whole reward to your address.

Mines around your work

Flexible mining gives a thread back for every core your other programs use, pauses when they fill the CPU and hands memory back when it runs low. Pick a power profile: performance, balanced or eco.

Wallets included

A Monero wallet and a Cryptoescudo wallet are created on first start and the miners pay into them. Receive, send, see the history, write down the recovery words. Keys stay on your device, encrypted.

Contacts you can trust

A contact is a public key with a short callsign, plus whatever you want to keep: wallet addresses, an I2P address, a website. Share your own card as a QR code, signed with your key so nobody can swap the addresses.

A chat room for every coin

People mining the same coin can compare notes in its room. No servers and no moderators: rooms travel over the app's own I2P node, and every member keeps the last two weeks, so newcomers see them too. Mute or hide anything on your own device.

Nothing to install besides the app

The I2P node, the Monero and Cryptoescudo nodes and the wallets are all part of the app, written in Dart and Flutter. It runs on Linux and Android today, and builds for Windows and macOS from the source.

Download

Android

Android 7.0 or newer. Phones mine Monero on the CPU; some phones can also use their GPU.

minerfan-android.apk
  1. Open the file on your phone and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
  2. minerfan is not on Google Play: store rules do not allow apps that mine on the device.

Linux

64-bit PC with GTK 3 (any common desktop). GPU mining uses your graphics driver's OpenCL.

minerfan-linux-x64.tar.gz 
tar xzf minerfan-linux-x64.tar.gz
mv minerfan ~/.local/opt/
~/.local/opt/minerfan/minerfan

On its first start it adds its own launcher and dock icon.

Checksums and older versions are on the releases page. Windows and macOS: build from the source.

Good to know

How you get paid

P2Pool has no pool wallet. Every share you find stays in the PPLNS window (about 6 hours on mini). Whenever a P2Pool miner finds a Monero block, its coinbase pays every wallet with shares in the window directly. The Rewards tab lists those payouts.

What is public

Chat messages are public: anyone in a coin's room can read them, and members keep them for 14 days. Your callsign is shown with each message. Mining itself talks only to the Monero, P2Pool and Cryptoescudo networks.

Power and heat

Mining uses a lot of power. It starts only when you switch it on, and the thermal control and power profiles keep laptops and phones from running hot.
